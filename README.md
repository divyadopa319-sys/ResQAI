# 🚨 ResQAI
### Evidence-Aware Emergency Routing and Resource Coordination

> **AI recommends. Human operators decide.**

ResQAI is an AI-assisted emergency coordination prototype designed to help operators respond to rapidly changing flood situations.

It transforms scattered emergency signals into an evolving decision-support workflow covering **evidence, risk, priority, resources, route safety, human approval, and field feedback**.

> ⚠️ **PROTOTYPE • SIMULATED EMERGENCY DATA**

---

## 🎯 Problem

Flood situations change continuously.

Emergency operators may receive:

- Citizen reports
- SMS / call information
- Weather alerts
- Field reports
- Changing water levels
- Route-condition updates
- Information about vulnerable people

A fixed priority list can become outdated as new evidence arrives.

---

## 💡 Solution

ResQAI demonstrates a closed-loop emergency coordination workflow:

```text
Emergency Signals
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
Risk Evolution
        ↓
Dynamic Priority
        ↓
Resource Recommendation
        ↓
Route Safety
        ↓
Human Operator Review
        ↓
Rescue Team
        ↓
Field Feedback
        ↓
Reassessment
